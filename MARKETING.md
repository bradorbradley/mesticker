# MeSticker — Marketing Masterclass

**Author:** Project lead (taking ownership)
**Audience:** Founder + future marketing hires
**Status:** Strategic playbook, ready to execute

---

## Thesis

MeSticker is a **photo → cartoon character → physical sticker sheet** product. The viral moment is the reveal: "holy shit, that's me as a chibi." The repeat-purchase moment is "I want one of my dog / my kid / my partner / my team."

The product is a **niche-printer**. The same 60-second flow serves dozens of buyer personas if the marketing speaks each one's language. AI lets us run all of them in parallel at near-zero creative cost.

The job: show the right person the right sticker on the right channel at the right emotional moment.

---

## Five strategic principles

1. **The character is the loop.** Once a user has their character, every variation is a new product. Every gift is a new customer. Every share is a billboard. Optimize for the first character generation, then the next 9 are gravity.

2. **Sell the moment, not the sticker.** Nobody wants a sticker. They want the laugh when they show it to their kid. The shareable iMessage. The water bottle they don't lose. Lead with the emotional payload.

3. **One niche per ad creative.** Don't make a generic "you as a cartoon" ad. Make 12 niche-specific ads. AI cost per creative ≈ $0.05. There's no excuse for generic.

4. **The physical product is also the marketing.** Every shipped sticker has a chance to be photographed and posted. Package it like it deserves to be unboxed. QR code on the back: "Make yours."

5. **Subscription is the endgame.** $14.99 one-time is fine. **$9.99/mo gets you a new themed pack every month** is a business. Build toward it.

## Niche Segments — twelve to launch with

For each segment: **Who · Hook · Channel · Creative angle · AI leverage · Lifecycle**

### 1. Pet Parents (the largest, easiest win)
- **Who:** Dog/cat owners, 25-55, active on Instagram and TikTok
- **Hook:** "Your dog deserves to be on every water bottle."
- **Channel:** Instagram + TikTok ads, dog-influencer DM partnerships, /r/dogpictures, Petfinder
- **Creative angle:** Side-by-side reveal video — actual dog photo → chibi dog with tail wagging. The vision-aware variations already do "WOOF!" speech bubbles, treat thought bubbles, and "no." cat thought bubbles. Lean in.
- **AI leverage:** Generate 50 sample dog characters from public stock photos for ad creatives. Run breed-specific creative variants ("Golden Retriever moms" vs "French Bulldog dads").
- **Lifecycle:** Anniversary of pet adoption → annual pack. Loss-of-pet memorial pack (sensitive — opt-in only). Multi-pet household = multi-character collection drive.

### 2. New Parents / Baby
- **Who:** Parents of kids 0-7, peak gifting moments around birthdays
- **Hook:** "Turn your kid's silliest face into stickers they'll hide in your bag forever."
- **Channel:** Instagram (mom influencers), Pinterest, TikTok #momlife, parenting newsletters
- **Creative angle:** Short before/after Reel — toddler photo → chibi version. Toddler's reaction to seeing themselves as a sticker. Birthday goodie-bag angle.
- **AI leverage:** Birthday-themed style preset ("Birthday Crown Chibi"). Holiday seasonal packs (Halloween costume version, Christmas elf version). Each becomes its own ad.
- **Lifecycle:** Each birthday = annual buy. Sibling jealousy = second character. Grandparent gift = upsell.

### 3. Teachers (high-margin segment)
- **Who:** K-12 teachers, especially elementary; year-end gift drivers
- **Hook:** "End-of-year stickers your students will fight over."
- **Channel:** Teachers Pay Teachers community, Pinterest, Facebook teacher groups, Instagram #teachersofinstagram
- **Creative angle:** Teacher's class roster turned into a sticker pack. "Bulk pack: 20 different students, one sticker each." This is the B2B SKU.
- **AI leverage:** Programmatic SEO landing pages: "/teacher-stickers", "/end-of-year-gifts-for-students", "/classroom-rewards"
- **Lifecycle:** September (back to school) + May (year end) — twice yearly seasonal spike. Multi-year customer (different class each year).

### 4. Couples / Partners (gift)
- **Who:** Anyone in a relationship buying a Valentine's, anniversary, or "thinking of you" gift
- **Hook:** "The cutest gift you can hand them tomorrow."
- **Channel:** TikTok #couplegoals, Instagram couple accounts, Reddit /r/relationship_advice gift threads
- **Creative angle:** Partner reaction videos (filmed with iPhone). "I gave him 15 stickers of myself..."
- **AI leverage:** Couple's-portrait style preset (two characters on one sheet). Date-night themed variations.
- **Lifecycle:** V-Day, anniversary, "just because." 4 spike moments per year per couple.

### 5. Sports Teams / Coaches
- **Who:** Coaches of kid sports, intramural teams, college club teams
- **Hook:** "End-of-season trophies, but stickerable."
- **Channel:** Facebook coach groups, TeamSnap partnership, Little League newsletters
- **Creative angle:** Team photo → roster of cartoon characters → bulk order
- **AI leverage:** Jersey-color and number prompt steering. "Coach pack" with personalized whistle and clipboard accessories.
- **Lifecycle:** End of every season per team. Multi-sport coaches = multiple buys per year.

### 7. Fitness / Gym

- **Lifecycle:** Quarterly offsites + annual holiday parties

## Cross-cutting AI marketing playbook

### A. Programmatic creative generation
For each niche above, run the following machine:
1. Generate 20 sample input photos from licensed stock images (or use real opt-in customer photos with permission)
2. Run them through MeSticker → 20 cartoon characters
3. Use those as the "before/after" hero in 20 niche-specific ads
4. **Total cost:** ~$2 per niche to produce 20 ad variants. Compare to $200+ for a single agency-produced ad.

### B. Programmatic SEO
Build landing pages per niche, each with:
- Hero: niche-specific headline ("Custom dog stickers in 60 seconds")
- Carousel: 6 sample stickers in that niche's style
- CTA: "Make yours"
- Social proof: 3 niche-specific testimonials (real or sourced)

Routes to ship: `/dog-stickers`, `/cat-stickers`, `/teacher-gifts`, `/wedding-favors`, `/baby-stickers`, `/pride-stickers`, `/gym-stickers`, `/streamer-stickers`, `/team-stickers`, `/birthday-stickers`, etc.

These cost ~1 hour each to scaffold. Index in Google Search Console. Drive organic traffic for evergreen niche queries.

### C. Personalized email sequences
Email captured? Tag the user with their first generation's style + vision-detected attributes. Automate:
- **Day 1:** Welcome + their sticker preview
- **Day 3:** "Your character looks great as a [variation]. Order more →"
- **Day 7:** "[Niche] users love the [seasonal pack]"
- **Day 30:** "Your character, but for [next holiday]"

Resend audience already wired in env. Just need the templates.

### D. Referral loop
Every order ships with:
- A QR code linking to a referral page
- "Make yours like [first name]'s. They get a free sheet when you order."
- The referred user's first cartoon shows the referrer's character as a "sample" → social proof + visual continuity

### E. Influencer micro-seeding
Send 100 free packs/month to micro-influencers (1k-50k followers) in each niche. Cost: $5/sheet × 100 = $500/mo. Track shares in PostHog via UTM. Expected reach: 5-50× ROI.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day roadmap

### Days 0-30 — "Get one niche profitable"
- Pick **Pet Parents** as launch niche (largest TAM, lowest barrier)
- Ship dog/cat-specific landing page (`/dog-stickers`)
- Run $50/day Instagram ads in Pet Parents niche
- Watch CAC vs AOV — get to break-even
- Build the email sequence for Pet Parents
- Manually seed 25 micro-influencer pet accounts

### Days 30-60 — "Layer in two more niches"
- Add **Teachers** + **New Parents** niches with their landing pages and creatives
- A/B test the variations sheet vs the single-design sheet ("Pack of Me" vs "Stack of Me") for repeat orders
- Launch the referral loop
- Add a paid digital sticker tier ($2.99 for HD downloads with extra effects)

### Days 60-90 — "Subscription + B2B"
- Launch **monthly themed pack subscription** ($9.99/mo, new theme every month)
- Open a B2B form for **bulk team / classroom orders**
- Start scaling ads in the niches that proved profitable
- Add 5-10 more niche landing pages based on traffic data
- First seasonal drop (e.g., Halloween pack)

## Metrics that matter

Track these in PostHog (most are already firing):
- **Hero conversion:** Visit → photo captured (target: 30%)
- **Reveal-to-cart:** Reveal viewed → first item added (target: 40%)
- **Cart-to-payment:** Cart opened → payment completed (target: 60%)
- **Email-capture rate** (target: 25%)
- **Repeat-purchase within 30 days** (target: 15%)
- **CAC by niche** (manage to <$10 for profitability at $14.99 AOV)
- **Time-to-first-sticker** (target: <90 seconds from landing)

## Why this is winnable

- **Generation cost** is dropping every model release. Margins improve with no work.
- **Variations + sheet hierarchy** create repeat-buy gravity that single-product competitors don't have.
- **Vision-aware steering** means every niche feels custom-made for that audience without manual work.
- **Programmatic creative** at $0.05/ad means we can outspend on creativity, not on ad budget.
- **Sticker-as-billboard** = every shipped order is a marketing impression.

The only real risk is **operational** — getting Printful fulfillment dialed, customer support responsive, and quality consistent. The marketing is winnable. The execution has to keep up.
